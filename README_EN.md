# ONCE DE DIAMANTES 1.0 ♦️

A small videogame born from imagination, curiosity, and the desire to tell a story.


## 🃏 The Story

In the **Reino Naipe 1.0**, cards are not merely pieces of a game.  
They are guardians, protectors, and citizens of an ancient realm where every figure has a purpose.

Tres de Trébol Negro (♣) and the Héroe (?), two cards, receive an unexpected mission:  
**to protect the kings of the kingdom.**

But a threat is approaching.

A dark group known as the **Once de Diamantes** has begun moving through the shadows.  
Their intentions remain unclear, but one thing is certain: the balance of the kingdom is in danger.

Now, our protagonists must prevent the kingdom from falling into chaos.


## 🎮 About the Project

**Once de Diamantes 1.0** is my first videogame and one of my first serious creative projects.

This project was born at university as part of a classroom assignment.  
A professor I deeply admired — and still admire — asked us to build a **simple card game using Java**.

However, while working on the assignment, I felt the urge to take the idea a little further… to create something **more in my own style**.

Instead of building a traditional card game, I began imagining a world where cards were characters, living in their own kingdom, facing conflicts and protecting their rulers.

That is how **ONCE DE DIAMANTES** was born.

What started as a small academic exercise eventually became the beginning of a story and my first step into creating original narrative worlds.
